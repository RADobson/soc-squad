# SOC Squad — Messaging & Positioning

## One-Liner
"AI-powered SOC agents that detect, investigate, and respond to threats 24/7 — on-prem, affordable, autonomous."

## Elevator Pitch
You're paying for Defender XDR and Sentinel but getting a fraction of the value. Alerts pile up, detections go untuned, and response takes hours instead of minutes. SOC Squad deploys four autonomous AI agents — XDR, SIEM, SOAR, and UEBA — that operate as your always-on Tier-1 SOC team. On-prem, so your data never leaves. For less than $5K/month instead of $600K/year in analyst salaries.

## Objection Handling

### "We already have an MDR provider"
"Great — how fast do they respond? Do you see exactly what they're doing? Can you customise their playbooks? SOC Squad runs on your network, you see every action, and it's customised to your environment. Many customers run SOC Squad alongside their MDR and find they can reduce their MDR tier (or drop it entirely) within 6 months."

### "AI can't replace human analysts"
"We agree — and that's not what SOC Squad does. It replaces the repetitive Tier-1 grind: alert triage, known-playbook response, log tuning. Your human analysts do what humans are best at: complex investigation, threat hunting, and strategic security decisions. SOC Squad makes your existing team 10x more effective."

### "What about false positives / AI hallucinations?"
"Every automated action has guardrails: confidence thresholds, human approval for high-impact actions, automatic rollback, and full audit trails. The bots are paranoid by design — they contain first and escalate rather than guessing. And because they run locally, you can review every decision in real-time."

### "We don't have Sentinel / Defender XDR"
"SOC Squad is optimised for the Microsoft security stack today because that's what most mid-market organisations have. We're building multi-vendor support (CrowdStrike, Splunk, Elastic) — but honestly, if you have E3/E5 licenses, you already have most of Defender. Let us help you turn it on."

### "This is too cheap — what's the catch?"
"No catch. AI agents cost us compute, not salaries. Our costs scale with tokens, not headcount. We pass that efficiency to you. The hardware is yours (or we help you buy it), so there's no vendor lock-in. If we're not delivering value, you stop paying."

## ICP Definition

### Ideal Customer Profile
- **Size:** 200-2000 employees
- **Industry:** Any regulated industry (finance, healthcare, government, legal)
- **Tech stack:** Microsoft 365 E3/E5, Defender XDR, Sentinel (or willing to deploy)
- **Current state:** Has security tools but under-utilises them; either no SOC or outsourced MDR
- **Budget:** Can allocate $3K-5K/month for security operations
- **Pain:** Alert fatigue, slow response times, compliance pressure, can't hire/retain analysts

### Anti-Patterns (Not a Fit)
- Enterprise with mature 20+ person SOC (they need tools, not agents)
- Startups with < 50 employees (not enough signal for UEBA, simpler tools suffice)
- Organisations with zero Microsoft security stack (too much lift to get started)
- Organisations that can't deploy on-prem hardware (regulatory or infrastructure constraints)
