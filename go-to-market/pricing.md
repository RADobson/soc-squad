## Land & Expand Strategy

1. **Land with XDR Bot** — immediate, visible value (alert fatigue reduction)
2. **Add SOAR Bot** — "your alerts are triaged, now let's automate response"
3. **Add SIEM Bot** — "response is automated, now let's improve detection quality"
4. **Add UEBA Bot** — "rules are tuned, now let's catch what rules can't"
5. **Cross-sell E8CR** — "your SOC is running, now let's get your compliance sorted"

## Pricing Philosophy

- Lower barrier to entry than E8CR (SOC value is more immediately tangible)
- XDR Bot is the wedge product — easiest to demonstrate ROI (before/after alert volumes)
- UEBA priced slightly lower — it's the most "nice to have" of the four
- Bundle discounts incentivise full squad adoption
- Cross-squad bundles create significant lock-in and switching cost
